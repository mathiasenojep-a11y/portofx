About Me

A passionate Full Stack Developer

I am a passionate Full Stack Developer with a unique blend of scientific knowledge, creativity, and technical expertise. I hold a Bachelor’s degree in Microbiology from Benue State University, Makurdi, where I developed strong analytical and problem-solving skills. Before transitioning into technology, I built over four years of professional experience as a photographer, capturing stories through creative visuals and developing a keen eye for detail, composition, and user-centered design. Driven by a passion for innovation and software development, I ventured into Full Stack Development two years ago. Since then, I have been dedicated to building responsive, scalable, and user-friendly web applications using modern front-end and back-end technologies. I enjoy turning ideas into functional digital solutions while continuously learning and embracing new technologies. Today, I combine my scientific background, creative experience, and programming skills to deliver high-quality digital products that are both visually appealing and technically robust. I’m always excited to collaborate on projects that solve real-world problems and create meaningful user experiences.
